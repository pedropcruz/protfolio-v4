::locale-content{lang="en"}
In a world drowning in gradient buttons and soft shadows, neo-brutalism emerges as a middle finger to the establishment. It's not about being ugly—it's about being **honest**.

## Why Everyone's Websites Look The Same

Figma templates. Bootstrap. Tailwind UI kits. We've optimized creativity out of existence. Every SaaS landing page is a clone of the last one that raised funding.

Neo-brutalism says: what if we stopped pretending? What if the border was actually visible? What if shadows were hard because shadows ARE hard?

## The Technical Philosophy

Border-radius: 0. Box-shadow: hard offset. Colors: high contrast. Fonts: bold, unapologetic. This isn't a design system—it's a rebellion.

But here's the secret: it's also more accessible. High contrast = better readability. Clear boundaries = easier navigation. Brutalism isn't just aesthetic—it's functional.

## Implementation Tips

- Use 4px borders consistently
- Shadows should be solid offsets (e.g., 4px 4px 0 black)
- Limit your color palette to 3-4 high-contrast colors
- Typography should be bold and uppercase for headers

## Porque Todos os Websites Parecem Iguais

## A Filosofia Técnica

Border-radius: 0. Box-shadow: offset duro. Cores: alto contraste. Fontes: bold, sem desculpas. Isto não é um design system—é uma rebelião.

Mas aqui está o segredo: também é mais acessível. Alto contraste = melhor legibilidade. Limites claros = navegação mais fácil. Brutalismo não é só estética—é funcional.

## Dicas de Implementação

- Usa bordas de 4px consistentemente
- Sombras devem ser offsets sólidos (ex: 4px 4px 0 black)
- Limita a paleta a 3-4 cores de alto contraste
- Tipografia deve ser bold e uppercase para headers
